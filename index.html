Computer Science and Mathematics at Southern Miss

SuramyaAngdembay

I'm an undergraduate researcher at The University of Southern Mississippi working on mechanistic interpretability, chain-of-thought faithfulness, and trustworthy AI. My research asks what language models actually do when we evaluate them, using correctness-stratified audits, sparse autoencoders, and causal interventions.

I work with Dr. Nick Rahimi on chain-of-thought faithfulness (CRA UR2PhD program), Dr. Haiyan Tian on a mechanistic audit of insider-threat language models, and Dr. Gregg Jacobs on error analysis of NOAA's RTOFS ocean forecasts. More about my research.
